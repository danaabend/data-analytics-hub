What changed for the business

In plain terms, not ticket numbers.

Trust & Quality

Cleared a backlog of 1,000+ stuck patient reports

1,000+ Body Comp reports were stuck in QA for a median of 30+ days, more than 3x longer than other services. We closed the loop with Giuliana's team on how these get actioned, clearing the backlog and giving us an accurate view of what's really outstanding.

Explore the update →
Trust & Quality

Caught silent care plan failures before they became longer patient delays

Through the Care Plan Progression workbook, we identified activities across Body Composition, Heart Health, and Brain Health Assessments that were ready for processing but had stopped progressing, some without appearing in the team's work-lists at all. Surfacing these cases prompted a deeper investigation with Clinical Operations and uncovered a broader workflow gap; Product has now scoped a new feature set to more deterministically distinguish services still pending, genuinely failed, or awaiting patient follow-up. Beyond preventing services from silently falling through the cracks, this gives Operations and Finance a stronger foundation for tracking order fulfillment and revenue recognition.
